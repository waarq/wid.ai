# 08. Open questions (owner decisions)

Each question has a recommendation and the cost of deciding wrong. Questions marked **(by M#)** block that milestone.

| # | Question | Recommendation | Cost of deciding wrong |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Q1 | **Self-hosted vs hosted AI** (ASR, diarization, LLM, embeddings)? **(by M5)** | **Hybrid behind provider interfaces.** Self-host ASR, diarization and embeddings (faster-whisper, pyannote, `bge-m3`) on an on-demand GPU. They are cheap, mature and keep raw audio in our infrastructure. Decide the **LLM** after the M6 bake-off. Default to self-hosted if `qwen3:14b`/`gpt-oss:20b` meet the eval targets. Otherwise use a hosted model under a DPA with no-training and zero/short-retention terms. Keep overflow routing to hosted off until the privacy policy allows it. | **All self-hosted too early:** GPU ops consume the single engineer, extraction quality may miss targets, and latency of 6 to 11 min vs 2 to 4 min. **All hosted:** customer audio and transcripts go to third parties, which undermines the "privacy-first" positioning, adds sub-processor disclosures and creates Google Limited Use exposure (R18). Switching later is cheap *because* of the abstraction. Prompts may need retuning per model (about 1 week). |
| Q2 | **GPU hosting** (serverless on-demand vs dedicated VM vs hyperscaler)? **(by M5)** | **On-demand / scale-to-zero GPU** (RunPod Serverless, Modal or similar) in the same region as Supabase until sustained utilisation exceeds about 40%, then a dedicated 24 GB box (vLLM for the LLM). Avoid hyperscaler GPUs at MVP (quota requests, price). | **Dedicated too early:** about $500 to 900/month idle. **Serverless too long:** cold-start latency and per-second premium at 5k+ meetings. Both are reversible in days because the sidecar is a stateless container. |
| Q3 | **Recording legality and consent UX** and **launch markets**? **(by M4, legal sign-off by M10)** | Keep `confirmBeforeCapture` **on by default** with the attestation checkbox, a copyable participant notice and no voice prints. Launch markets are chosen with counsel. Put the consent obligation in the ToS. Do not market "secret" or "invisible" recording. | **Too little friction:** potential criminal or civil exposure for users and WIT in all-party-consent jurisdictions, regulatory action under GDPR, reputational damage. **Too much friction:** lower capture rates. Friction is cheap to tune later; liability is not. |
| Q4 | **Multi-member orgs now or later?** **(by M2)** | **Schema now, minimal product now.** `org_id` on everything, a personal org per user, plus **email invitations to one company org** (owner/admin/member, a single team label) in M2 so "Team Calls" and "team" visibility mean something. Defer SSO/SAML, SCIM, domain capture, multi-org switching UI and per-org billing. | **Later (no org_id now):** retrofitting tenancy means rewriting every RLS policy and back-filling every table, about 3 to 5 weeks and high leak risk. **Full enterprise orgs now:** 4+ weeks spent on features no early user needs. |
| Q5 | **Data residency / region?** **(by M0, for prod by M10)** | Choose **one** prod region close to the first paying customers, with GPU availability nearby. If EU customers are targeted, choose an EU region (Frankfurt) from the start. A non-EU first market can use its nearest region. Document the region in the privacy policy. Multi-region is out of scope. | Moving a Supabase project between regions later is a full migration (dump/restore, storage copy, downtime, new OAuth redirect hosts), about 1 to 2 weeks plus risk. Losing EU deals over residency is worse. |
| Q6 | **Billing and plan limits?** **(design by M6, implement post-MVP)** | No billing in the MVP. Meter now: `processing_runs.cost_estimate_usd`, captured minutes per user per month, assistant questions. Decide plan limits (minutes/month, max meeting length, history length) **before public launch**, because cost scales per audio minute. Stripe later. | Launching without limits invites cost blow-ups (R15). Adding metering after the fact means guessing historical usage. |
| Q7 | **Zoom app: publish publicly or keep it private?** **(by M9)** | Build and test as an unpublished app in our own Zoom account, then **submit for Marketplace publication** in M9 (needed for any external user). Keep scopes read-only and minimal to ease review. | Not publishing means only our own account can use Zoom import. Over-broad scopes mean a rejection-and-resubmit cycle of several weeks. |
| Q8 | **Email notifications at MVP?** **(by M8)** | In-app alerts plus Realtime only for the MVP. Add a transactional email provider (Postmark, Resend or SES) in M8 **only** for share invitations to non-users and the weekly summary if the owner wants them. Requires SPF/DKIM/DMARC. | **Without email:** invited non-users never learn they have access (sharing to external people looks broken). **With email too early:** deliverability work and a spam-abuse vector (invite rate limits are in [03-security-rls.md](./03-security-rls.md) section 11). |
| Q9 | **Permission details the mock leaves open:** who may edit action items, who sees deals, what "team" means | (a) Action items: owner **and** assignee may update or toggle; only the owner may delete. The mock lets any viewer edit. (b) Deals: org-wide read; owner or admin write. (c) "Team" visibility = all members of the meeting's org. (d) Recipients may not reshare (`allowRecipientsToReshare` default false is enforced). | **Too open:** viewers silently change others' work, causing trust issues. **Too closed:** assignees can't tick off their own tasks, which defeats action tracking. Changing it later is a policy plus UI copy change (days), unless data was already corrupted by over-permissive edits. |
| Q10 | **Show the transcript when AI extraction fails?** (the mock hides it) | **Yes.** The meeting status is `failed` with Retry, and the transcript (if committed) stays readable. | Hiding it makes a pipeline hiccup look like data loss and produces support tickets. Very low cost either way, so mostly UX. |
| Q11 | **"Anyone with the link" sharing?** | Keep it **off by default** (org flag `link_sharing_available=false`, matching the mock). When enabled: read-only brief, transcript only if `share_transcript`, audio only if `share_recording`, 30-day default expiry, revocable, access logged. | **On by default:** accidental public exposure of sensitive meetings, which is the worst kind of incident for this product. **Never available:** friction for sharing with external clients. It can be enabled later safely. |
| Q12 | **Ship `capture_mode='video'` at MVP?** | **No.** Audio and transcript-only first. Video costs about 50x the storage, needs Safari transcoding and adds little to the core value (decisions and actions). Revisit on demand. | Shipping it early adds storage cost and playback bugs. Not shipping risks losing users who want screen-share context, which is mitigated by "upload a recording". |
| Q13 | **Monorepo (move the web app to `apps/web`) vs a separate `wit-backend` repo?** **(by M0)** | **Monorepo** with a shared `packages/contracts` (see [01-architecture.md](./01-architecture.md) section 4). | **Separate repos:** contract drift caught only at runtime, plus a versioned package to publish. **Monorepo:** a one-time move PR (about 1 day). |
| Q14 | **Default recording retention?** | **90 days** for playback audio (matches mock settings), transcripts and insights kept until deleted. Users can set "keep until deleted". | **Too short:** users lose audio they expected to replay, which feels like data loss. **Too long or forever:** storage cost and a larger breach impact. Changing the default affects only new recordings. |
| Q15 | **Languages at launch?** | **English officially.** Urdu/English and other languages "best effort, beta" until the eval shows acceptable WER and extraction quality. Auto-detect per meeting. | Advertising unsupported languages leads to bad first impressions. Excluding them loses the local market the demo persona represents. |
| Q16 | **Interrupted captures that are never resumed:** auto-process or auto-discard? | **Auto-process after 24 h** with a `meeting_ready` alert. It respects manual capture, because the user already captured the audio. | Auto-discard silently destroys recordings. Asking forever leaves limbo meetings stuck in `paused`. |
| Q17 | **Which hosted providers, if any** (ASR, LLM, embeddings)? **(by M5/M6)** | Shortlist providers that offer a DPA, no training on API data, regional processing matching Q5 and JSON-schema outputs. Pick one per role. Avoid multi-vendor sprawl. | A provider without suitable terms blocks EU customers and Google Limited Use compliance. Too many vendors increase sub-processor and maintenance load. |
| Q18 | **Desktop agent or bot vendor priority?** | Do not start before M9 data exists. Measure the share of mic-only and upload-fallback captures by platform. Above 30%, start a desktop agent spike (macOS first). Consider a bot vendor only if users explicitly want "send WIT to the meeting". | **Starting early:** 6 to 10 weeks on signing, notarisation and two audio stacks before product-market fit. **Starting late:** Mac + Zoom-desktop users (likely a large segment) get mic-only notes and churn. |
| Q19 | **Assistant scope:** single-meeting only (current interface) or cross-meeting at MVP? | Single-meeting at MVP (as built in the UI). Cross-meeting designed in M7 and shipped after launch behind a flag, needing `POST /v1/assistant/ask`. | Shipping cross-meeting early adds the hardest RAG quality and permission-filtering work to the critical path. Never shipping it leaves the PRD's "searchable meeting memory" weaker than competitors. |
| Q20 | **Frontend interface additions** listed in [02-data-model.md](./02-data-model.md) section 16 (Topic traceability, key-point sources, `discardCapture`, upload service, speaker assignment, new alert types, `audioUrlExpiresAt`)? | Approve them as **additive** changes during M2 to M5, landing one PR per milestone together with the API change. | Without them: insights that can't be traced (Topic, keyPoints), no way to fix wrong speakers, missing capture flows. With them done carelessly: frontend churn. Additive fields keep the risk low. |
